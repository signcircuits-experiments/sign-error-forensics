## Declaration

Status: **FROZEN 2026-08-28** — predicted direction confirmed by the author
(mean(A1−A2) > 0). Content-final before any pod execution. One run, reported
regardless of outcome. No edits to this section after freezing.

- **Site:** L66_mlp (control: L30_mlp).
- **Data:** held-out IBP corrects, n=50 (all minus-written). No det cases.
- **Procedure:** identical to the existing s09 arm A (A1 = error-mean patch,
  A2 = self-mean patch; teacher-forced; same pipeline stage, site swapped).
- **Provenance disclosed:** L66 was selected post hoc from the held-out s02 DLA
  scan (36/36 vs 3/75). This run tests it on new *causal* measurements.
- **Prediction (judge hypothesis):** mean(A1−A2) > 0 at L66_mlp — same
  direction as det's L79 judge (+1.48, 36/36).
- **Pass bars (all must hold):**
  1. mean(A1−A2) > 0 at L66_mlp;
  2. ≥ 75% of the 50 cases individually positive;
  3. broken ≤ 5% at every site (muted-judge clause);
  4. L30_mlp |mean gap| ≤ 0.5.
- **Optional secondary (descriptive, no bar):** arm B boosts at L66 on the 26
  held-out errors; a real muted judge should fix ≤ 5% at γ ≤ 2.
- **Interpretation rules, fixed now:** pass ⇒ L66 is reported as the IBP judge,
  found by disclosed exploration and confirmed causally; fail in reverse
  direction ⇒ L66 joins L63 as amplifier-like, and the paper reports that IBP
  has no identified judge; fail as null ⇒ L66 is a correlate only. No other
  outcomes will be claimed.
- **One-shot rule:** a single execution on the pod; no reruns, no site or
  hyperparameter changes after this file is frozen.

## Outcome (added post-execution, 2026-08-29 — the frozen sections above are unedited)

Executed once, per the one-shot rule. Result files:
`Qwen/heldout_IBP/s09_judge/ibp_correct_judge_candidates_armA_L66.json` and
`ibp_error_judge_candidates_armB_L66.json`; run log alongside this file.

- Bar 1 (mean(A1−A2) > 0 at L66_mlp): +0.0275 — nominally met.
- Bar 2 (≥ 75% of 50 cases positive): 23/50 = 46% (16 negative, 11 zero) — **FAILED**.
- Bar 3 (broken ≤ 5% at every site): 0/50 at every site/arm — met.
- Bar 4 (L30_mlp |mean gap| ≤ 0.5): 0.005 — met.

All four bars were required. **Verdict: MISS, as a null** (mean gap +0.03,
sign-test p ≈ 0.34, 95% CI [−0.02, +0.08]; compare L63's −1.46 and det L79's

and the paper reports that IBP has no identified judge.** Secondary arm B
(descriptive): boosting L66 fixes 0/25 errors at every γ ∈ {1.5, 2, 3}
(n=25, not 26: the one embedding-bias case is excluded upstream, exactly as in
the adjudicated L63 arm-B run). No reruns were or will be performed.
